# 📊 Growth-Metrics-Dashboard | Power BI

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Organizations often deal with large volumes of raw sales data that are **unstructured and difficult to interpret**. Without proper analysis, it becomes challenging to identify **sales trends, customer behavior, and profit-driving factors**.
This creates a gap in **data-driven decision-making**, leading to missed opportunities for growth and optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To develop an interactive and visually appealing **Power BI dashboard** that converts raw data into **actionable insights**, helping stakeholders monitor key business metrics such as **sales, profit, orders, and customer trends**.

---

## 🚀 Aim

The aim of this project is to design a centralized dashboard that enables:

* Quick tracking of **business performance (2019–2021)**
* Identification of **high-performing regions and categories**
* Analysis of **customer contribution and purchasing patterns**
* Support for **strategic and data-driven decisions**

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard creation & visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning & transformation
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI & measure creation
* **Excel** – Dataset handling
* **Data Modeling** – Relationship building

---

## ⚙️ Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Data Collection

* Imported raw sales dataset containing **orders, customers, products, and regions**

### 2. Data Cleaning

* Removed null and duplicate values
* Ensured data consistency across fields

### 3. Data Transformation

* Converted data types (text → numeric, date formatting)
* Standardized values and categories
* Rounded numerical values to **2 decimal precision**
* Created calculated columns for better analysis

### 4. Data Modeling

* Built relationships between tables (fact & dimension tables)
* Ensured proper schema for accurate filtering and aggregation

### 5. DAX Implementation

* Created KPIs such as:

  * Total Sales
  * Total Profit
  * Total Orders
  * Customer Count

### 6. Visualization

* Designed an interactive dashboard with filters, slicers, and charts
* Enabled dynamic analysis across **regions, categories, and time**

## 🔍 Key Insights (Data-Driven)

* 📍 **West Region** contributed the highest share of sales (~32%), followed by East (~29%), showing strong regional performance
* 📉 Sales experienced a **decline of ~15–20% during September–October**, indicating seasonal slowdown
* 📈 Sales peaked in **December**, contributing the highest monthly revenue due to year-end demand
* 🪑 **Office Supplies** generated ~60K sales (~38% of total), making it the top-performing category
* 💳 **Online payments dominated (~41%)**, followed by COD and card payments, highlighting customer preference for digital transactions
* 👤 Top 10 customers contributed **40%+ of total profit**, indicating high dependency on key customers
* ⚠️ Profit fluctuations observed in early 2020 suggest external business or market impact
